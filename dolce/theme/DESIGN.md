# Dolce Editorial

## Brand overview
Dolce Lingerie sells lingerie, bodysuits, corsets and everyday underwear packs in Colombia. The experience is for women selecting garments on their phones. It should feel personal, confident, tactile and easy to shop.

## Design thesis
The intimacy of a fashion editorial, with the clarity of a well-organized fitting room.

## Typography
Poppins 400 and browser-synthesized 500/600 for body, controls and prices, loaded through Shopify font_picker, swap. Body 16px/1.6, labels 14px, secondary card metadata 12px. Georgia 400 with Times New Roman serif fallback for editorial headings. H1 fluid 40–96px, H2 32–64px, line-height 1.07, tracking -.035em. No italic headline swaps. Brand logo uses the existing Dolce image.

## Spacing and composition
Desktop max 1552px, 56px gutters; tablet 32px; mobile 20px. Vertical sections 88px desktop and 55px mobile. Four-column desktop products and two-column mobile products. Product gallery uses 57/43 desktop and a native horizontal strip mobile. Corners square, no ornamental shadows, thin functional dividers. Hero uses two existing portrait photographs desktop and one mobile.

## Components
Primary button wine/white 52px high, square, 14/28px padding. Secondary button ink outline. Cards use edge-to-edge 3:4 photographs, a quiet title/price row, real price and availability. Header centers the real logo; menu and search remain compact. Footer blush, large lowercase wordmark and factual contact/navigation. Option groups use native radios and labeled swatches. The mobile buy bar preserves price, availability and safe-area padding. Guide and zoom use native dialogs.

## Dos and Don'ts
Do keep prices, stock, photos and product descriptions dynamic from Shopify. Do preserve current product URLs. Do keep content editable in section settings. Do show true shipping terms, no fake reviews or stock urgency. Do not replace garment evidence with generated photography. The AI still life is ambient brand imagery only. Do not use generic benefit cards, floating ornaments, gradients as branding or an animated LCP image.

## Motion philosophy
Native scrolling and touch first. Press feedback .97 at 150ms, drawer/zoom transitions 200–300ms, only secondary editorial reveals at 480ms. Transform and opacity only. No autoplay video, scroll hijacking or heavy 3D. Honor prefers-reduced-motion.

## Reference lock
Primary: user Pinterest pin 1151725304739129229, editorial lingerie photography, soft blush/white canvas, clear collection discovery. Secondary: Intimissimi and SKIMS for buying hierarchy, fit information and clear options; Lounge for complete-the-look context. User's web-creative-skills guide native touch, short motion and functional feedback. Preserve the image-led rhythm, generous alignment and product-first buying flow. Reject template filler, fabricated reviews and speculative product claims.
