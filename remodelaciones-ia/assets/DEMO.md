## Antes — prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural.
Asset type: BEFORE image for a residential remodeling comparison slider.
Primary request: A completely believable ordinary slightly outdated Colombian middle-class apartment living room before renovation, photographed horizontally in 3:2 aspect ratio, no people or text.
Scene: modest rectangular living room, roughly 5 by 4 metres, 2.5-metre flat ceiling. View from a fixed front corner showing the entire room. On the LEFT part of the rear wall is one wide rectangular aluminum-framed window, roughly 2 metres wide, sill 70 cm above the floor, with beige curtains opened to both sides and a muted view of Colombian urban residential buildings. On the RIGHT part of the same rear wall is one standard dark-brown wooden doorway opening into a dim corridor. These two fixed openings must both be clearly visible. The continuous RIGHT wall is a plain TV wall, with a 43-inch black wall-mounted television above a cheap low dark laminated TV cabinet. A tired simple gray fabric three-seat sofa runs along the LEFT wall, facing the TV. Small low wood coffee table, simple faded rug. Old terracotta-beige square ceramic floor tiles with visible grout, yellowish off-white painted walls, simple white ceiling with one dated central light fixture. Everything tidy, lived-in, economically ordinary, not distressed or ruined.
Style: convincing architectural real-estate photograph, authentic surface texture, natural soft daylight, neutral white balance, no exaggerated glamour, 24mm full-frame lens, camera height 1.4m, straight vertical lines.
Composition: horizontal 3:2, fixed full-room corner view, include the whole window, whole doorway, ceiling junctions and floor. Window and doorway must be spatial anchors.
Constraints: no people, no writing, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no split screen, no collage, no dramatic impossible perspective, no luxury finishes.

Los tres niveles cambian acabados y muebles sin ampliar la habitación ni mover los huecos. La variante esencial conserva el piso cerámico; integral cambia a madera; autor incorpora piedra y nogal.
